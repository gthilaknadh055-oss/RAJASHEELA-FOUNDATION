ABOUT US

Who We Are

Rajasheela Foundation identifies, honors, and rewards top-performing employees and deserving students across all departments and categories.

  • Employee Excellence: Honoring dedicated professionals across railways, education, healthcare, corporate, and public service.
  • Student Awards: Supporting academic merit, innovation, and perseverance across all disciplines.
  • All Categories: Ensuring frontline workers, educators, and young achievers receive the recognition they earn.
OUR VISION

Uplifting communities by celebrating extraordinary dedication and hard work,
while inspiring a new generation of leaders across every sector.
