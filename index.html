About Us

RAJASHEELA FOUNDATION is voluntary organisation Society committed to recognizing and honouring talented individuals and organizations whose dedication, achievements and service create a positive impact on society. Through the Foundation Awards, we aim to celebrate meaningful contributions across a wide range of fields and encourage others to serve with commitment and excellence.
